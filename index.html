For precision layout fidelity, vector graphic formats are highly recommended. We accept:
  • Vector Formats (Preferred): PDF, AI (Adobe Illustrator), EPS, or SVG.
  • Raster Images: High-resolution PNG or JPG files (minimum 300 DPI, white/transparent backgrounds).
If you do not have design templates, our technical layout specialists can assist in digitizing files for production.
Standard manufacturing timelines once layouts are locked and sample approvals are finalized:
  • Production: 7 - 10 working days inside the Tirupur factory.
